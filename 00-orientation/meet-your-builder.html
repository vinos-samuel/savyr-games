Quick reference

Ground rules

You're the boss. Claude builds what you imagine — you decide what's fun.
Small steps win. One tiny piece at a time beats one giant piece that never works.
Changing your mind is allowed. That's not a mistake — that's the whole point of the loop.
Build a little, play a little. We stop and play after every piece — that's how you know what's next.
